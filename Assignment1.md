## Difference Between KDD and Data Mining

| KDD (Knowledge Discovery in Data) | Data Mining |
|---|---|
| KDD is the complete process of finding useful knowledge from data. | Data Mining is one step of the KDD process. |
| It includes data cleaning, selection, transformation, mining and evaluation. | It focuses on finding patterns, relationships and useful information from data. |
| KDD is a broader process. | Data Mining is a part of KDD. |
| Its goal is to discover meaningful and useful knowledge from raw data. | Its goal is to discover hidden patterns and trends in data. |
| It involves the entire knowledge discovery process. | It uses techniques like classification, clustering and association. |
| Example: Discovering useful knowledge about customer buying behavior through the entire process. | Example: Finding that customers who buy bread also frequently buy milk. |
